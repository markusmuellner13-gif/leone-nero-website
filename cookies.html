Cookie Policy

Information about the cookies we use, why we use them, and how you can control them.

Last updated: 1 June 2025  |  Compliant with EU Cookie Directive & GDPR

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners. Cookies may be "session cookies" (which expire when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (which remain on your device for a set period).

We also use similar technologies such as local storage, pixel tags, and web beacons, which are collectively referred to as "cookies" in this policy.

2. How We Use Cookies

Leone Nero S.r.l. uses cookies on our website (leonenero.com) and within our software applications for the following purposes:

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function and cannot be disabled. They include:

  • Authentication tokens: to keep you logged in to your account;
  • Session management: to maintain your session state;
  • Security cookies: CSRF protection and fraud detection;
  • Cookie consent: to remember your cookie preferences.

Legal basis: These do not require consent as they are necessary for the service to function.

2.2 Functional / Preference Cookies

These cookies allow the website to remember your preferences:

  • Language and region preferences;
  • Display settings (dark/light mode);
  • Personalisation of content.

Legal basis: Consent (opt-in). If you decline, the website will still function but some preferences may not be remembered.

2.3 Analytics Cookies

We use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website. This helps us improve the user experience. We use privacy-conscious analytics tools and, where possible, anonymise IP addresses. Analytics data is never sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

  • Pages visited and navigation paths;
  • Time spent on pages;
  • Device and browser type;
  • Referral source.

Legal basis: Consent (opt-in).

2.4 Marketing / Advertising Cookies

We currently do not use third-party advertising networks or retargeting cookies on our website. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated and your consent will be sought before any such cookies are placed.

4. How to Manage and Control Cookies

4.1 Cookie Banner

When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie consent banner allowing you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the site.

4.2 Browser Settings

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Here are links to cookie management instructions for popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Please note that blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of our website, including the ability to log in to your account.

4.3 Opt-Out of Analytics

You can opt out of analytics tracking by declining analytics cookies in our cookie banner. If you prefer a browser-level opt-out, many analytics providers offer browser plugins or opt-out mechanisms.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Please re-visit this policy regularly to stay informed. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect when the latest changes were made.

If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact us at privacy@leonenero.com.
