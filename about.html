Our Story

The Black Lion
Behind the Code

We are Leone Nero — an independent software house built on craft, conviction, and the refusal to ship anything less than exceptional.

Our Mission

Software Built to Last

Leone Nero S.r.l. was founded with a singular purpose: to create software that earns its place on your device. In a world saturated with disposable apps and bloated tools, we chose a different path — precision over speed, quality over quantity, and craft over shortcuts.

The name Leone Nero — Italian for "Black Lion" — reflects our philosophy. A lion does not compromise. It is focused, powerful, and unhurried. That is the energy we bring to every product we release.

From day one, we have operated as a lean, senior-only team. We don't hire juniors to inflate headcount. Every engineer, designer, and strategist on our team has shipped real products to real users at scale.

Core Values

What We Stand For

⚡

Performance First

We profile before we optimise, and we optimise before we ship. No bloat, no excuses. Our software is fast on day one and stays fast at scale.

🎨

Intentional Design

Every interaction, every transition, every pixel has a reason. We believe great design is invisible — it just works, and it feels right.

🔒

Security by Default

We never treat security as a feature to add later. Privacy-first architecture and zero-knowledge patterns are built into our core engineering standards.

🤝

User Trust

We earn trust through transparency, honest changelogs, fair pricing, and software that does exactly what we say it does — nothing more, nothing hidden.

🧪

Rigorous Testing

Every release passes automated testing, internal QA, and real-device validation. We chase edge cases so our users never encounter them.

🌍

Global Mindset

Accessibility, i18n support, and inclusive design are not optional. We build software for the world, not for a subset of it.
