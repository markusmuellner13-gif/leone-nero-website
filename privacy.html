Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Last updated: 1 June 2025  |  Effective: 1 June 2025  |  Applies to EU/EEA users under GDPR

1. Who We Are and How to Contact Us

Leone Nero S.r.l. ("Leone Nero", "we", "us") is the data controller responsible for your personal data. Our details:

  • Company: Leone Nero S.r.l.
  • P.IVA: IT12345678901
  • Address: Via della Tecnologia 12, 20100 Milano (MI), Italy
  • Data Protection Contact: privacy@leonenero.com

2. What Personal Data We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account data: name, email address, username, password hash;
  • Purchase data: billing name, billing address, payment method (payment card data is processed by our payment processor — we never store full card numbers);
  • Communications: messages, support tickets, and feedback you send us;
  • Profile data: optional profile picture, display name, preferences.

2.2 Data We Collect Automatically

  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, session duration, crash reports;
  • Device data: device type, operating system, browser type and version, screen resolution;
  • Network data: IP address, approximate location (country/city derived from IP), referrer URL;
  • Cookies and tracking: see our Cookie Policy for details.

2.3 Data from Third Parties

If you log in using a third-party service (e.g., Google, Apple Sign-In), we receive your name and email address from that provider, subject to the permissions you grant.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Provide and improve our Services (legal basis: contract performance / legitimate interest);
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions (legal basis: contract performance);
  • Send transactional emails such as receipts, security alerts, and service updates (legal basis: contract performance / legitimate interest);
  • Send marketing communications with your consent — you can opt out at any time;
  • Analyse usage patterns to improve product performance and UX (legal basis: legitimate interest);
  • Comply with legal obligations such as tax record-keeping, fraud prevention, and responding to law enforcement requests (legal basis: legal obligation);
  • Safety and security — detecting and preventing abuse, spam, and security incidents (legal basis: legitimate interest).

4. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Art. 6(1)(a) — Consent: Marketing emails, non-essential cookies;
  • Art. 6(1)(b) — Contract: Account management, purchase processing;
  • Art. 6(1)(c) — Legal obligation: Tax compliance, law enforcement;
  • Art. 6(1)(f) — Legitimate interest: Fraud prevention, product analytics, security.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Service providers: Cloud hosting (AWS/GCP), payment processing (Stripe), analytics (self-hosted or privacy-first tools), email delivery services — all bound by data processing agreements;
  • App Stores: Apple and Google receive transaction data for in-app purchases;
  • Legal authorities: If required by law, court order, or to protect rights;
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger or acquisition, under appropriate confidentiality.

6. International Data Transfers

If we transfer your data outside the EEA, we do so under appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission or an adequacy decision.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide our Services and comply with legal obligations. In general:

  • Account data: retained while your account is active and for 30 days after deletion;
  • Purchase records: retained for 10 years to comply with Italian fiscal law;
  • Support communications: retained for 3 years;
  • Analytics data: retained in anonymised form after 26 months.

8. Your Rights

Under GDPR and applicable Italian law, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data we hold;
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • Restriction — request that we limit processing of your data;
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest or direct marketing;
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@leonenero.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. In Italy, this is the Garante per la Protezione dei Dati Personali (garanteprivacy.it).

9. Children's Privacy

Our general Services are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16 without verifiable parental consent. Some of our games may be rated for all ages; in those cases, parental consent is required before in-app purchases and we minimise data collection for younger users in accordance with the relevant app store policies.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational security measures, including encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.3+), access controls, regular security audits, and anomaly detection. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes by email or prominent website notice and update the "Last updated" date. Continued use of our Services after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights: privacy@leonenero.com
